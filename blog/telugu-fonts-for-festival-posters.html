Best Telugu Fonts for Festival Posters

Fonts that feel celebratory without looking cheap — for Dasara, Deepavali, Sankranti and Ugadi posters and WhatsApp greetings.

8 min read·September 22, 2026·By Akshay

Telugu households send and share a lot of festival wishes — Vinayaka Chavithi, Bathukamma, Dasara, Deepavali, Sankranti and Ugadi all fill WhatsApp groups with greetings and posters. The font decides whether a poster feels festive and warm or cheap and crowded.

What Makes a Good Festival Font

  • Celebratory, not childish — decorative but still readable
  • Heavy enough for gold, orange and red on busy backgrounds with lamps, rangoli and flowers
  • Clear conjuncts — festival names like సంక్రాంతి and శుభాకాంక్షలు are full of them

Layout Tips

  • One decorative font + one plain font. Festival name in Ponnala or Ravi Prakash; the wish line or sender's name in Mandali or Noto Sans Telugu.
  • Give text a backing. A soft dark glow or a translucent band keeps gold text readable over photos.
  • Leave space for the family name. "మీ … కుటుంబం" at the bottom in a smaller, simpler font.
  • Square for WhatsApp, vertical for status. 1080×1080 for chats and Instagram posts, 1080×1920 for status and stories.
  • Check the spelling. శుభాకాంక్షలు is misspelled surprisingly often in forwarded posters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Telugu font is best for festival wishes?

Ponnala is the best all-rounder. For a softer, personal greeting use Suravaram, and for devotional festivals try Dhurjati.

What size should a WhatsApp festival poster be?

1080×1080 pixels for chats and Instagram posts, and 1080×1920 pixels for WhatsApp status and stories.

Can I use these fonts on posters for my shop?

Yes. They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use.
