Unicode vs ASCII Telugu Fonts

Why some Telugu text survives copy-paste and some turns into gibberish — explained without jargon.

7 min read·September 8, 2026·By Akshay

Why does some Telugu text survive copy-paste perfectly while other Telugu text turns into Ôá´ó£ Hõ-style gibberish? The answer is the difference between Unicode and ASCII (legacy) fonts. Once you understand it, most Telugu font problems make sense.

Two Different Ideas of "Telugu Text"

Unicode: the letters are real

Unicode gives every Telugu character its own permanent number. క is always U+0C15, ా is always U+0C3E. Telugu has its own block, U+0C00–U+0C7F. A Unicode font simply knows how to draw those numbers — so any Unicode Telugu font on any device can display the same text.

ASCII / legacy: the letters are disguised

Legacy fonts were made before computers understood Telugu. They reuse the numbers of English letters and symbols and draw Telugu shapes there instead. The file stores "English"; only the special font makes it look Telugu.

How to Tell Which One You Have

  1. Change the font. Select the text and switch it to Noto Sans Telugu or Nirmala UI. Unicode text stays Telugu; legacy text turns into English letters and symbols.
  2. Paste it into Notepad or WhatsApp. If you see Telugu, it's Unicode.
  3. Check the font name. Gautami, Vani, Nirmala UI, Noto, Mandali, Ramabhadra, Tiro Telugu and all Google Fonts Telugu fonts are Unicode. Anu and many older DTP fonts are not.

Why Legacy Fonts Existed At All

In the 1990s and early 2000s, most design software could not arrange Telugu vowel signs and conjuncts on its own. Legacy fonts sidestepped that: the typing program placed every piece manually, so the result looked perfect in PageMaker or CorelDRAW. That's why printers adopted them — and why so many old wedding cards, books and notices still exist only in legacy files.

Today, Windows, macOS, Android, iOS and every modern browser shape Unicode Telugu correctly, so that workaround is no longer needed.

Which Should You Use?

Unicode, for everything new. It's the only kind of Telugu that works on the web, on phones, in search, in email and in future software. Keep legacy fonts only when an existing print workflow truly depends on them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gautami Unicode or ASCII?

Gautami is a Unicode font, as are Vani, Nirmala UI and every Telugu font on Google Fonts.

Can I convert ASCII Telugu to Unicode?

Yes, but you need a converter built for the specific legacy font family the text was typed in, because each family maps letters differently. Always proofread the result.

What is the Unicode range for Telugu?

Telugu occupies the block U+0C00 to U+0C7F, which includes all vowels, consonants, vowel signs, the virama, Telugu digits and fraction signs.
