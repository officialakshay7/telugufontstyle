How to Use Telugu Fonts in Canva

Telugu fonts that work inside Canva, uploading custom fonts on Canva Pro, and fixing broken Telugu text.

6 min read·September 12, 2026·By Akshay

Canva is the go-to design tool for Telugu posters, invites and Instagram posts — but Telugu text can be fiddly in it. This guide shows how to find Telugu fonts in Canva, how to add your own on Canva Pro, and what to do when letters look broken.

Step 1 — Get Telugu Text Into Canva

Canva doesn't have its own Telugu keyboard. Either:

  • Type with your device's Telugu keyboard (Gboard on Android, the Telugu keyboard on iPhone, or Telugu Phonetic on Windows), or
  • Type in English on our Telugu typing tool, copy the Telugu output, and paste it into a Canva text box.

Always paste Unicode Telugu. Text copied from an old DTP file (Anu and similar) will paste as English gibberish.

Step 2 — Choose a Telugu Font in Canva

Select the text, open the font list and search by name. Canva's library changes over time, so availability varies, but it's worth searching for these Google Fonts Telugu families:

If a font you pick doesn't contain Telugu, Canva either shows boxes or quietly switches to a fallback font. When the style suddenly looks plain, that's usually why.

Step 3 — Upload Any Telugu Font (Canva Pro)

  1. Download the font from Google Fonts — for example NTR, Suravaram or Ponnala
  2. In Canva go to Brand (Brand Kit) → Brand fonts → Upload a font
  3. Upload the .ttf or .otf file and confirm you have the right to use it (Google Fonts' OFL allows this)
  4. The font now appears at the top of your font list in every design

Step 4 — Fixing Broken Telugu in Canva

Vowel signs or conjuncts look separated

Try a different font first — Noto Sans Telugu is the most reliable. If one uploaded font shapes badly, the file may be incomplete; download it again from Google Fonts.

Letter spacing breaks words apart

Canva's letter-spacing slider works between characters. Heavy positive spacing can pull Telugu vowel signs away from their consonants visually. Keep spacing at 0 for Telugu.

Can't get a specific decorative look

Use our font generator: type your text, pick a style with 3D, outline or shadow, click ⬡ Transparent to download a transparent PNG, and upload it to Canva as an image. It works on the free Canva plan too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Canva support Telugu fonts?

Yes. Canva can display Unicode Telugu, and its library includes some Telugu-capable fonts. Availability changes, so search by font name. Canva Pro users can upload any Telugu font.

How do I type Telugu in Canva on a laptop?

Add the Telugu Phonetic keyboard in Windows or the Telugu input source on Mac, or type in English on our Telugu typing tool and paste the result.

Can I use Google Fonts Telugu fonts in Canva designs I sell?

Yes. Fonts under the SIL Open Font License can be used in commercial designs.
