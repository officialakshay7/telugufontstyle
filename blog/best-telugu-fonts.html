12 Best Telugu Fonts of 2026

Ranked by category — display, sans-serif, serif and handwriting. With use-case recommendations and free Google Fonts download links.

10 min read·September 1, 2026·By Akshay

Choosing the right Telugu font is one of the most noticeable design decisions you can make for anything written in Telugu. The typeface sets the mood — traditional, modern, playful or formal — before anyone reads a word.

Below are the 12 Telugu fonts I reach for most often, grouped by category. Every one of them is free, open-source and available on Google Fonts, so you can use them for personal and commercial work without paying anything.

Category 1 — Display Fonts

Display fonts are built to be seen big. Use them for headlines, posters, YouTube thumbnails and social media graphics where impact matters more than long-form readability.

1. Ramabhadra — Best for: bold headlines & thumbnails
తెలుగు వెలుగు

Round, heavy letterforms with a uniform stroke and small temple-shaped terminals. Ramabhadra was designed for headlines and posters, and it is the safest choice when text has to stay readable over a busy photo. Download →

2. Ponnala — Best for: festive & decorative posts
పండగ శుభాకాంక్షలు

Thick, decorative and full of personality. Ponnala works beautifully for festival greetings, shop banners and anything that should feel celebratory. Keep it for short lines — it gets heavy in long text. Download →

3. Baloo Tammudu 2 — Best for: friendly, versatile display
సంతోషం ఆనందం

The Telugu member of Ek Type's Baloo family. Rounded and warm, with weights from Regular to ExtraBold, it moves from headline to short body text better than most display faces. Great for kids' content, food brands and YouTube. Download →

4. Ravi Prakash — Best for: invitations & eye-catching titles
ఆహ్వాన పత్రిక

A decorative display face meant for very large sizes on screen. On print it holds up at smaller sizes too. Use it for event titles, invitation headings and posters. Download →

Category 2 — Sans-Serif Fonts

Sans-serif fonts are the workhorses of digital Telugu — clean, legible at small sizes, and comfortable for body text on phones and laptops.

5. Noto Sans Telugu — Best for: everything digital
డిజిటల్ తెలుగు

Google's Noto Sans Telugu has multiple weights and widths and complete character coverage. It is the default Telugu font on Android, so it is the most familiar Telugu letterform for millions of readers. When in doubt, start here. Download →

6. Hind Guntur — Best for: websites, apps & UI
మన తెలుగు భాష

Part of Indian Type Foundry's Hind project, in five weights from Light to Bold. It was designed for user interfaces, with flat stroke endings that keep letters clear at 14–18px. Download →

7. Mandali — Best for: news & long reading
నేటి వార్తలు

Designed for news publications and named after Mandali Venkata Krishna Rao, who organised the first World Telugu Conference in 1975. Very readable in paragraphs and rich in Telugu conjunct forms. Download →

Category 3 — Serif Fonts

Serif Telugu fonts carry a literary, traditional feel — the first choice for books, magazines, certificates and anything that should look printed rather than typed.

8. Tiro Telugu — Best for: books & literature
కవిత్వం సాహిత్యం

Tiro Telugu began life as a typeface for the Murty Classical Library of India. It follows the proportions of the Telugu manuscript tradition and has lovely stroke contrast — ideal for poetry, books and premium editorial design. It even has an italic. Download →

9. Suranna — Best for: editorial headlines
ప్రత్యేక కథనం

A news-publication font with a distinctive heavy base on its letters. Suranna looks confident in magazine headings and section titles. Download →

Category 4 — Handwriting & Calligraphy Fonts

Handwriting fonts add warmth and a personal touch — perfect for quotes, greeting cards, wedding stationery and social posts.

10. NTR — Best for: artistic, hand-lettered titles
అందమైన అక్షరాలు

A handwriting font inspired by the famous lettering style of artist Bapu, and named after N. T. Rama Rao. It instantly gives Telugu text a classic, artistic flavour. Download →

11. Suravaram — Best for: brush-script greetings
శుభోదయం

A brush-script Telugu font that looks written with a loaded brush. Lovely for greetings, quotes and invitation accents. Download →

12. Gurajada — Best for: casual quotes & posters
మంచి మాట

A relaxed Telugu handwriting font named after the reformist poet Gurajada Apparao. Good for motivational quotes and informal posters. Download →

How to Choose the Right Telugu Font for Your Project

A font that looks stunning on a poster can be completely wrong for a website, and a beautiful book face can look weak in a thumbnail. Three quick checks will save you from most mistakes.

Match Font Personality to the Message

Ponnala and Ravi Prakash feel festive. Ramabhadra feels loud and confident. Tiro Telugu feels literary. NTR feels artistic and nostalgic. Before choosing, ask what feeling the text should create — then pick the font whose personality matches it.

Match Weight to Size

Telugu letters have small internal loops and a lot of detail below the line (vattulu). At small sizes, thin weights close up and become hard to read. For body text below 14px, prefer Regular or Medium weights of Noto Sans Telugu, Hind Guntur or Mandali, and keep light or decorative fonts for large headings.

Test Conjuncts and Vowel Signs in Your Actual Text

Always type your real headline before deciding. Words with stacked consonants such as "శ్రీ", "క్ష్మ", "స్త్ర" or the ru-vowel sign in "కృష్ణ" reveal a font's quality immediately. If the subscript forms look cramped or collide with the next line, choose another font or increase the line height.

Common Telugu Font Mistakes

Using legacy (non-Unicode) fonts for new work

Many DTP centres still use older fonts such as the Anu series. Text typed in them is stored as English characters, so it breaks on websites, in WhatsApp and on other computers. For any new project, use a Unicode Telugu font. Our Anu fonts guide explains the difference.

Too many fonts in one design

Two fonts is plenty — one for headlines, one for body text. Two weights of the same family (for example Hind Guntur Bold and Regular) count as one decision and always look tidy.

Tight line spacing

Telugu needs more vertical room than English because of vowel signs above and conjuncts below. A line height of 1.6–1.8 for paragraphs avoids letters touching between lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Telugu font is best for beginners?

Noto Sans Telugu. It renders every letter and conjunct correctly, works at every size, and is already the default on Android. Once you are comfortable, try Hind Guntur for screen work and Ramabhadra or Baloo Tammudu 2 for display work.

Can I use these Telugu fonts for commercial projects?

Yes. All fonts listed here are on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use with no fees — client work, printed products, apps and anything you sell.

Which Telugu font is pre-installed on Windows?

Windows includes Nirmala UI, which covers Telugu, and the classic Telugu fonts Gautami and Vani (on Windows 10 and 11 these two come with the Telugu Supplemental Fonts feature, which is added when you install Telugu as a language). See our Gautami font guide.
