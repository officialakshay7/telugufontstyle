Best Telugu Fonts for Wedding Cards & Invitations

Which Telugu fonts work for a subhalekha, how to split fonts across the card, and the mistakes to avoid.

8 min read·September 10, 2026·By Akshay

A Telugu wedding card — the subhalekha (శుభలేఖ) — is often the first thing guests see of the wedding. The font does a lot of the work: it has to feel auspicious and elegant, print cleanly on card stock, and still look good when the card is photographed and forwarded on WhatsApp.

How a Telugu Wedding Card Is Usually Structured

Most cards follow a familiar order, and each part suits a different kind of font:

Card sectionExampleFont type that works
Blessing line at the topశ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తుTraditional serif or decorative
Headingకళ్యాణ మహోత్సవ ఆహ్వానంDecorative display

Family names & RSVP—Clean sans or serif, smaller

How to Split Fonts Across the Card

Use two fonts, three at most:

  • One decorative font for the heading and names (NTR, Suravaram or Ravi Prakash)
  • One readable font for everything else (Tiro Telugu, Mandali or Noto Serif Telugu)
  • Optionally, a traditional font for the blessing line only (Dhurjati)

Bilingual card? Many Telugu families print English on the reverse. Match the mood: a Telugu calligraphy font on the front pairs well with a classic English serif such as Playfair Display on the back.

Mistakes to Avoid

Tiny decorative text

Decorative Telugu fonts lose their subscripts (vattulu) at small sizes. Never use them for the address or muhurtham details.

Not proofreading names

Names often have complex conjuncts — క్ష్మి, శ్రీ, ద్ర. Print a test page, and have an elder check spellings and the muhurtham time. A wrong letter in a name is the most common card error.

Using legacy fonts for the digital version

If the card was typeset in a non-Unicode DTP font, the text can't be reused for a WhatsApp invite or a wedding website. Ask your printer for a Unicode version, or keep the PDF/image for sharing.

Low contrast on photos

For the WhatsApp video or image invite, gold text on a busy background looks lovely in print but disappears on a phone. Add a soft shadow or a darker band behind the text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Telugu font is best for the bride and groom names?

NTR for a classic, Bapu-style look, or Suravaram for a brush-script feel. Both are free on Google Fonts.

Can I use these fonts on cards I print commercially?

Yes. All the fonts mentioned are under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial printing.

How do I make a Telugu wedding invitation for WhatsApp?

Design it in Canva or Photoshop using Unicode Telugu fonts, then export as an image or video. You can also download styled text from our generator as a transparent PNG and place it on your design.
