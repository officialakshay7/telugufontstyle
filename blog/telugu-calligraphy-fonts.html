Best Telugu Calligraphy & Handwriting Fonts

Brush, pen and hand-lettered Telugu fonts for invitations, quotes and posters — with live previews and free downloads.

8 min read·September 3, 2026·By Akshay

Hand-lettered Telugu has a long history — from palm-leaf manuscripts to the famous lettering of artist Bapu that shaped the look of Telugu film titles, book covers and magazine headings for decades. Calligraphy and handwriting fonts bring some of that warmth to digital text.

Here are the best free Telugu calligraphy and handwriting fonts, what each one is good for, and how to use them without making your design look cluttered.

The Best Telugu Handwriting & Calligraphy Fonts

1. NTR — Bapu-inspired lettering
శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు

Inspired by Bapu's lettering and the many artists who followed his style. NTR is the font to use when you want a classic, artistic Telugu look for titles, invitations and posters. Download →

2. Suravaram — Brush script
శుభోదయం మిత్రమా

A true brush-script font with varied stroke width. Beautiful for greetings, quotes and festival wishes. Download →

3. Gurajada — Relaxed handwriting
మంచి మనసు

Casual and friendly, named after the reformist poet Gurajada Apparao. Ideal for quotes, captions and informal posters. Download →

4. Lakki Reddy — Playful display handwriting
సరదా సమయం

Bold, bouncy handwriting with lots of personality. Great for kids' content, food menus and fun social posts. Use it for short phrases only. Download →

5. NATS — Handwriting for headings
నా కథ

A handwriting font for headings, posters and decorative invitations. Its simpler strokes stay legible at smaller sizes than most script fonts. Download →

6. Sirivennela — Light calligraphic touch
వెన్నెల రాత్రి

A newer addition to Google Fonts with a delicate, light stroke. Works well for poetic lines and elegant accents over images. Download →

7. Timmana — Decorative display
ఉత్సవం

Not handwriting exactly, but its lively decorative strokes pair well with calligraphy fonts for big titles. Download →

Tips for Using Calligraphy Fonts Well

  • Keep them short. A name, a greeting or a single line. Paragraphs in script fonts are tiring to read.
  • Pair with a plain font. Use Noto Sans Telugu, Mandali or Tiro Telugu for dates, addresses and details.
  • Go big. Handwriting fonts need at least 28–32px on screen to show their character.
  • Check conjuncts. Type the actual names — లక్ష్మి, కృష్ణ, శ్రీనివాస్ — and look at the subscripts before you print.
  • Give it room. Increase line height; script vowel signs often reach further above and below the line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free Telugu font in Bapu's lettering style?

NTR on Google Fonts is inspired by Bapu's lettering style and the artists who followed it. It is free under the SIL Open Font License.

Can I use these calligraphy fonts on a wedding card I print and sell?

Yes. The SIL Open Font License allows commercial use, including printed products. You just cannot sell the font file itself.

Why does my calligraphy text look normal after pasting into WhatsApp?

Fonts do not travel with copied text — WhatsApp displays Telugu in the phone's own font. Save the styled text as an image (PNG) from our generator to keep the look.
