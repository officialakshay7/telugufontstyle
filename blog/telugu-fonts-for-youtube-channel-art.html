Telugu Fonts for YouTube Channel Art & Banners

Pick a Telugu font for your channel name, banner, profile picture and intro so it looks consistent everywhere.

7 min read·September 16, 2026·By Akshay

Your channel name appears in more places than you think — the banner, the profile picture, the intro, the watermark, end screens and community posts. Picking one Telugu font (or one pair) and using it everywhere is what makes a channel look like a brand instead of a collection of videos.

Building a Simple Channel Type System

  1. Pick one family with several weights (Baloo Tammudu 2, Hind Guntur, Anek Telugu or Noto Sans Telugu). Use the heaviest weight for your channel name.
  2. Optional accent font — a handwriting font (NTR, Suravaram) only for taglines or one word.
  3. English partner — if you also write English, choose a Latin font with a similar feel. Several Telugu fonts on Google Fonts include their own Latin letters, which match automatically.
  4. Write it down — keep the font names, weights and colours in a note so every thumbnail and banner stays consistent.

Banner Tips

  • Keep all text inside the centre safe area (1546×423 px) — outer parts are cropped on phones.
  • Channel name in Telugu, upload schedule in smaller text (ప్రతి శుక్రవారం కొత్త వీడియో).
  • Check that subscripts aren't cut off at the bottom of the safe area.
  • For the profile picture, use just the first Telugu letter or a short mark in a heavy font.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is a YouTube banner?

Upload 2560×1440 pixels and keep text and logos inside the central 1546×423 pixel safe area, which is visible on all devices.

Should my channel name be in Telugu or English?

If your audience searches in Telugu, a Telugu name builds recognition; many channels use Telugu on the banner and English in the channel title for search. Keep both in fonts with a similar feel.

Are these fonts free for a monetised channel?

Yes. They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use.
