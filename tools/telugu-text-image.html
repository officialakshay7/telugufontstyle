Telugu Text to Image — What It's For

This tool converts your Telugu text into a downloadable PNG image with your chosen font, size, and background colour. Perfect for creating WhatsApp DP images, Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnail text, or any graphic that needs Telugu text without using Photoshop or Canva.

What formats can I download?

PNG with your chosen background colour, or transparent PNG (no background) for overlaying on other images in Canva, Photoshop, or CapCut.

How do I use this for YouTube thumbnails?

Type your thumbnail text in Telugu, choose a bold font (Ramabhadra or Baloo Tammudu 2 work best), set a contrasting colour, download transparent PNG, then import into Canva or Photoshop over your thumbnail background.

Can I use these images on WhatsApp?

Yes — download the PNG and send it directly in WhatsApp as an image. Or set it as your DP by saving to your phone gallery first.
