Why Use a Telugu Word Counter?

Standard word counters often miscount Telugu text because they don't recognise Telugu Unicode correctly. This Telugu word counter is specifically built to handle conjunct consonants (vattulu), vowel signs (guninthalu), and the space-based word boundaries of Telugu script — giving you accurate counts every time.

Use it for checking essay word limits, optimising Instagram captions, counting characters for the WhatsApp About line (139 limit), or making sure your Telugu blog posts hit their target word count.

Does this counter work for both Telugu and English?

Yes — it handles mixed Telugu-English text correctly, counting words and characters accurately regardless of script.

How does it count Telugu words?

Telugu words are separated by spaces, just like English. This counter splits on spaces, so conjuncts and vowel signs always stay part of the same word. Note that Telugu joins many words together (sandhi), so a Telugu text often has fewer, longer words than its English translation.

Why does my Telugu text have more characters than letters?

Every vowel sign, pollu (్) and sunna (ం) is a separate Unicode character. "నమస్తే" looks like 4 letters but is 6 characters — and platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram and X count those 6. This counter shows the same Unicode count.
