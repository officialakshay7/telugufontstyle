About These Tools

Every tool here produces standard Unicode Telugu, so the text you create works in WhatsApp, Instagram, Word, Google Docs and on any modern phone or computer. Most tools run entirely in your browser — your text is not uploaded anywhere. The translation tool sends your text to Google Translate to get the translation, and voice typing uses your browser's speech recognition service.
