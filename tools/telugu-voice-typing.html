Telugu Voice Typing — Speak and Get Text

This Telugu voice typing tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to convert spoken Telugu into Telugu text. No app installation, no API key, no cost — it works entirely in your browser using the Web Speech API available in Chrome.

Speak naturally in Telugu and the text appears in real time. You can also mix Telugu and English in the same sentence. After transcription, copy the text to use anywhere, or click "Style It" to see it in 100+ Telugu font styles.

Which browser works best for Telugu voice typing?

Google Chrome on desktop or Android. Chrome has the best Telugu (te-IN) speech recognition support. Firefox and Safari do not support the Web Speech API fully.

Is my voice data saved anywhere?

We never record or store your voice, and nothing is sent to our servers. Note that Chrome's built-in speech recognition may send the audio to Google to transcribe it, under Google's privacy policy — the same as voice typing on an Android keyboard.

Can I use this for dictating long documents?

Yes — the continuous mode keeps listening until you press stop. You can dictate paragraphs and the text accumulates in the output box. Copy it to Word or Google Docs when done.
