Telugu to English Translation — Free Online

Translate Telugu text to English instantly with this free online tool. Type or paste Telugu text and get an English translation in seconds. Works for words, sentences, and paragraphs. Type English instead and it translates into Telugu automatically.

Is this Telugu to English translation accurate?

The translation engine is powered by Google Translate, which provides high quality Telugu-English translation. For formal or professional translation (legal, medical), always have a human translator review the output.

Can I translate English to Telugu too?

Yes — just type English in the left box and the tool detects it and translates into Telugu. The Swap button moves the translation back into the input box.

Is there a word limit for translation?

The free API works best for text up to about 500 words. For longer documents, translate in sections. For full document translation, Google Translate's website handles any length.
