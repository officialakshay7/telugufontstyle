Do Telugu fonts work for Kannada text?

No — Telugu and Kannada use separate Unicode blocks, so a font needs Kannada characters to display Kannada. A few fonts, such as Akaya (Kanadaka for Kannada, Telivigala for Telugu), come in matching versions for both scripts.

Which font is best for Kannada?

For body text: Noto Sans Kannada or Hind Mysuru. For display and headings: Baloo Tamma 2, Hubballi or Padyakke Expanded One. For a traditional, literary look: Tiro Kannada or Noto Serif Kannada.
