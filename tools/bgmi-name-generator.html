Do these stylish names work in BGMI?

Most mobile games accept Unicode names, but support for Telugu letters and rare symbols varies between games and updates. If a game rejects the name or shows boxes, tick "Keep English letters" and use a symbol style.

How long can a BGMI name be?

BGMI names have a short character limit, and in Telugu every vowel sign and pollu counts as a separate character — "చరణ్" is 4 characters, not 2 letters. Keep to one short word plus a small symbol; the counter under each style shows its length.

Can I use these names on other games too?

Yes — COD Mobile, Garena Free Fire, PUBG Mobile, and most games support Unicode names. If a style doesn't paste correctly, try a simpler style without emoji symbols.
